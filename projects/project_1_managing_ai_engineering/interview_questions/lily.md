# Interview questions for Lily

**Full conversation available [at this link](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ade3893-87ec-83e8-87d3-2262c4c1e003)**

- What are your short-term and long-term goals for the website?
- How will you judge whether the site is successful, and what measurable improvements would you
  like to see compared with today?
- Which parts of handling orders, managing inventory, and answering customer questions cause the
  most frustration today? Can you describe a recent example?
- Who will use the site, including customers and staff, and what does each group need to accomplish?
- Which capabilities are essential for launch, and which could wait for a later version?
- Could you walk me through an ideal customer visit, from arriving on the site to receiving an
  order, including what the customer should see at each step?
- What products and services should customers be able to order online, and what choices or details
  are needed for custom arrangements or event orders?
- Will customers pay directly on the site, pay in person, or have both options? Which payment
  methods should be available?
- If a payment fails or the customer leaves checkout before finishing, what should happen to the
  order, any reserved inventory, and the customer's progress?
- What delivery and pickup options should customers have, including service areas, fees, cutoff
  times, and available dates or time slots?
- If a requested delivery time is unavailable, a delivery fails, or an order is delayed, what
  should the customer be told and what alternatives should they have?
- How should customers request order changes, cancellations, or refunds, and what restrictions
  should apply once preparation or delivery has begun?
- Could you walk me through how staff should receive, prepare, and complete an order, including
  how inventory should change along the way?
- If an item becomes unavailable before or after checkout, how should substitutions or refunds
  work, and when must the customer approve a change?
- What confirmations and order-status updates should customers receive, through which channels,
  and how should they check progress themselves?
- Should customers be able to check out as guests, create accounts, or both? What should an
  account let them do that a guest cannot?
- Do you offer promotions or a rewards program, and what rules should the site apply when a
  customer tries to use an expired, invalid, or incompatible offer?
- What tools currently handle your website, payments, orders, inventory, and customer records,
  and which need to connect to the new site or remain in use?
- What pages, product details, photos, and shop policies should appear on the site, and who will
  provide and keep that information up to date?
- What needs should we accommodate for customers using phones, assistive technology, or
  languages other than English?
- Which customer questions or tasks should the AI assistant handle around the clock? Could you
  give an example of a conversation that ends successfully?
- What information should the AI use for its answers, who will keep it current, and does it need
  access to live inventory or order information?
- If the AI cannot answer, gives an incorrect answer, or a customer requests a person, how should
  it respond and arrange follow-up, especially outside business hours?
- What customer information may the AI access or collect, how should it verify someone before
  discussing an order, and which actions must require staff approval?
- What launch date, budget, and operational constraints should guide the project, including
  expected normal and peak order volumes?
